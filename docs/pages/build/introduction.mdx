### Product Blueprint: Soulpad (Developer: Skillfreek)
**Soulpad** is an Apple-grade, high-engagement Bible knowledge and story-driven learning app built for iOS and Android. It blends cognitive scenario-based trivia, interactive scripture stories, high-stakes game show mechanics, and skill certification into a seamless, dark-mode experience for all demographics—from casual learners and families to working professionals and Christian leaders.
### Mobile Architecture & Toolchain Integration
**React Native Framework (reactnative.dev)**
 * Cross-platform UI layer leveraging Reanimated 3 for fluid 60fps micro-animations, glassmorphic rendering layers, and native audio bridge support.
**Monetization & Conversion Engine (superwall.com + revenuecat.com)**
 * **Superwall:** Controls dynamic, remotely configured paywalls triggered at high-intent moments (e.g., streak milestones, level completion, lifeline top-ups) without app store resubmissions.
 * **RevenueCat:** Manages cross-platform in-app subscriptions, product entitlement states, server-side receipt validation, and real-time LTV/ARPU analytics.
**Growth, UGC & Lifecycle Marketing (sideshift + atio.com)**
 * **Sideshift:** Operational platform to recruit, brief, and compensate UGC creators who generate high-converting video hooks based on real app gameplay.
 * **Atio (atio.com):** Lifecycle CRM orchestration engine powering behavior-driven push notifications, personalized re-engagement campaigns, and custom streak recovery flows.
### Design System & Visual Blueprint
 * **Base Canvas:** Deep Midnight Indigo (#080A15) with radial background glows (#161B3D).
 * **Glassmorphic Cards:** Translucent dark panels (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)) with fine light borders (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)) and native backdrop blur filters (blur(20px)).
 * **Accents:** Burnished Gold (#E6B800) for primary actions/rewards; Emerald Glow (#10B981) for correct answers; Crimson Crimson (#EF4444) for high-stakes timer alerts.
 * **Typography & Audio:** Apple's native *SF Pro Display/Text*, paired with atmospheric, spatial ambient audio loops and tactile haptic feedback on every tap.
### Core Gameplay & Learning Mechanics
**1. The Ascent (High-Stakes Solo Mode)**
 * 15-question ladder modeling *Who Wants to Be a Millionaire*.
 * **Dynamic Lifelines:** *Scriptural Hint*, *50:50*, *Verse Swap*, and *Community Wisdom*.
 * **The Council's Offer (Banker Mechanic):** At milestone safety nets (Questions 5 & 10), "The Council" offers players a guaranteed cash-out offer in Skillfreek Tokens/Pearls to walk away or risk their current run to scale the next tier.
**2. Scripture & Event Learning Modules**
 * Interactive, episodic story arcs covering key Biblical eras, historical events, and parables with short-form Q&As at the end of each module.
**3. Competitive Leagues & Skill Trees**
 * Mastery ladders from *Initiate* to *Cherubim Tier*, rewarding daily play with verified Skillfreek digital badges and certificates.

### Monetization & Retention Playbook
**Soulpad Pass Subscription Tiers**
 * **Offerings:** $7.99/month, $49.99/year, or $99.99 Lifetime Access.
 * **Benefits:** Zero ad interruptions, unlimited daily campaign attempts, access to deep theological commentaries, family profile sharing, and exclusive monthly Skillfreek certifications.
**UGC Recruitment & Growth Loop (Sideshift)**
 * Recruit faith creators, teachers, and trivia enthusiasts via Sideshift to produce reaction-style TikTok/Reels content showcasing high-stakes "Council Offers" and intense question showdowns. Direct links point directly to Superwall onboarding flows.
**Behavioral CRM Workflows (Atio)**
 * **Streak Protection Alert:** Triggered via Atio 4 hours before daily reset if the user hasn't played: *"Your 5-day Faith Streak is expiring tonight. Protect your rank on the Cherubim Ladder."*
 * **Post-Loss Re-Engagement:** Sent 10 minutes after a player fails a high-level Ascent run: *"The Council has unlocked a bonus lifeline for your next attempt. Re-enter the Ascent."*
